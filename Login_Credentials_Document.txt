How to Log In
1. Open the application in the browser.
2. Go to: http://localhost:3000/login
3. Enter the username and password for the chosen account.
4. Click Log In.

Notes
- The admin account is used to accept or reject assignment submissions.
- The student account is used to submit assignments and view grades.
- Keep these credentials secure.
